The Open Door Design Community

A place to learn, contribute, test ideas, share lived experience, and help build technology that works for more people.

Open Door Design is built in public, and the Community is how that work becomes a conversation instead of a broadcast. Members follow new work, test what is being built, and help shape what Open Door Design becomes next.

Why join the Community?

Community members can follow new work as it is built, participate in testing with their own assistive technology, explore educational resources, share accessibility feedback, and connect with projects that match their interests.

Screen reader users, keyboard users, low vision and magnification users, voice input users, Deaf and hard of hearing community members, developers, designers, educators, and curious visitors are all welcome.

Ways to participate

  • Learn through the Accessibility Academy.
  • Test Innovation Lab projects with real assistive technology.
  • Share screen reader, keyboard, low vision, cognitive, hearing, or other accessibility perspectives.
  • Join research and collaboration opportunities.
  • Volunteer or explore a partnership with Open Door Design.
  • Receive updates about new tools and community activities.

What registration asks

Only a first name, last name, and email address are required to join. Questions about interests, accessibility perspectives, participation preferences, and a short introduction are optional.

Open Door Design does not sell Community information. Read the Community Privacy Notice for the full details before registering.

Join the Community

Registration takes just a few minutes, and every question beyond your name and email is optional.

Already a member?

Community members can sign in without a password. Enter the email address used during registration and the Community will send a secure, single-use sign-in link. The link expires after 15 minutes. A successful sign-in creates a seven-day member session and opens the protected Community Dashboard.
