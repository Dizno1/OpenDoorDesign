Contact

Start the Right Conversation

Open Door Design is in its founding season, and conversation is one of the ways this work grows.

Whether you want to report an accessibility barrier, ask a general question, discuss a collaboration, or share an idea, the best way to begin is by choosing the email address that matches your reason for reaching out.

Accessibility Questions

Use this address for accessibility barriers, usability feedback, screen reader concerns, keyboard access issues, requests for accessible alternatives, or questions about accessibility support.

Accessibility@OpenDoorDesign.org

General Information

Use this address for general questions about Open Door Design, the website, the Academy, resources, or information that does not fit one of the other contact categories.

Info@OpenDoorDesign.org

Collaboration and Partnerships

Use this address for project ideas, partnership discussions, speaking opportunities, research collaboration, organizational interest, mentoring, or ways to support the work of Open Door Design.

Inquiries@OpenDoorDesign.org

What to Include

When possible, include a brief description of what you are contacting us about, the page or project involved, the assistive technology or browser used if relevant, and the best way to follow up.

You do not need to write a perfect message. A simple explanation is enough to begin the conversation.

Response Expectations

Open Door Design is still developing, so response times may vary.

Accessibility-related messages are especially important. We will make every reasonable effort to review accessibility feedback, understand the issue, and improve the experience where possible.

The Door Is Open

Open Door Design was built from conversations, mentorship, shared experience, and people willing to open doors for others.

If you have a question, idea, barrier, or opportunity to discuss, we welcome the conversation.
