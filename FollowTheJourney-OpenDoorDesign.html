Follow the Journey

Open Door Design is being built in public, one tested improvement at a time. This page tracks the progress of the projects, the lessons learned along the way, and what is coming next.

Currently Building

CPACC Accessibility Academy

The CPACC Accessibility Academy is complete. All five domains are fully built, and the course now provides comprehensive lessons designed to teach accessibility concepts in depth rather than present brief study summaries. Future work will focus on maintenance, refinement, and continued quality review.

CPACC Accessibility Lab

The CPACC Accessibility Lab is mostly complete and now includes more than 450 questions. Its Reinforce, Practice, Challenge, and Sprint experiences are in place, with additional questions and refinements continuing to expand the learning experience.

WCAG Accessibility Lab

The WCAG Accessibility Lab is mostly complete. Its core learning and challenge experience is working, and additional scenario-based questions will continue to be added so learners can practice applying WCAG principles to realistic situations.

Beepball Practice Caller

The Beepball Practice Caller is mostly complete. The application is functioning and is now waiting for additional recordings that reflect the current team's preferred calls and voices.

Media Workflow Assistant

The Media Workflow Assistant is the newest active project. It can inspect a selected media file, identify available actions, and run goal-oriented workflows such as preparing media for artificial intelligence. Future source support will include media URLs as well as local files.

Recent Milestones

  • The CPACC Accessibility Academy was completed across all five domains.
  • The CPACC Accessibility Lab grew to more than 450 questions, with additional questions still being developed.
  • The WCAG Accessibility Lab reached a mostly complete state, with more scenario-based questions planned.
  • The Beepball Practice Caller reached a mostly complete state and is waiting for additional team-preferred recordings.
  • The Media Workflow Assistant completed its first end-to-end workflow, including inspection, recommendation, execution, and Markdown file generation.
  • The Open Door Design website was updated to reflect the current status of the project ecosystem.

What We Are Learning

The strongest work happens when accessibility is treated as the foundation rather than a final inspection. Screen reader testing, keyboard behavior, focus management, meaningful headings, clear instructions, and flexible interaction are being tested as the projects grow.

The Academy work has also established a new writing rule: lessons must teach, not merely cover a topic. Each section should help learners understand why a barrier exists, how it affects people, how professionals evaluate it, and how the concept connects to real accessibility practice.

What Comes Next

Immediate priorities include expanding the CPACC Accessibility Lab beyond its current collection of more than 450 questions, adding more scenario-based questions to the WCAG Accessibility Lab, adding the preferred team recordings to the Beepball Practice Caller, and continuing development of the Media Workflow Assistant through additional real workflows and source options.

This page will continue to grow as milestones are completed, new challenges are discovered, and the community contributes ideas and feedback.
