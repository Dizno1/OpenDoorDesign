Inquiries

Contact Open Door Design

Open Door Design welcomes inquiries related to accessibility engineering, usability evaluation, accessibility education, assistive technology support, accessible media, and inclusive design strategy.

How to Reach Out

For now, email is the preferred contact method while the public launch version of OpenDoorDesign.org is being prepared.

Email Contact@OpenDoorDesign.org

Please include the goal of the inquiry, any relevant links, the type of accessibility support needed, and any timeline that may be important.

Common Inquiry Types

Accessibility Review

Website, document, media, or workflow review involving screen reader usability, keyboard navigation, WCAG issues, and practical recommendations.

Training and Education

Support for accessibility learning, screen reader awareness, WCAG practice, usability concepts, and inclusive design training.

Accessible Media

Discussion of audio description, accessible video workflows, media editing accessibility, and non-visual access to multimedia content.

Accessibility Collaboration

Project conversations involving accessibility engineering experiments, prototypes, accessibility labs, and inclusive technology concepts.

Report an Accessibility Barrier

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site or in an Open Door Design project, please include the page or feature, the assistive technology used, the browser if known, and the task that could not be completed.
