Accessibility Commitment

Accessibility Is Our Foundation

At Open Door Design, accessibility is not an afterthought. It is not a feature added at the end of a project. It is the foundation for how we think, learn, design, build, test, and improve.

Technology should create opportunity, not barriers.

Every page, lesson, prototype, and project is shaped by the belief that accessibility should support independence, participation, and belonging.

Screen Reader First

Open Door Design follows a Screen Reader First philosophy.

Before asking how something looks, we ask how it works for someone who cannot see the screen.

Can it be understood? Can it be navigated? Can it be completed independently? Can it be completed efficiently?

These questions guide the way we build. When an experience works well for screen reader and keyboard users, it often becomes clearer, simpler, and more usable for everyone.

WCAG 2.2 AA and Real-World Usability

Open Door Design uses WCAG 2.2 AA as a primary benchmark for accessibility work.

WCAG provides an important framework, but true accessibility goes beyond passing automated checks or meeting technical requirements on paper.

Real accessibility is measured by whether real people can complete real tasks using the assistive technology, devices, browsers, and workflows they rely on every day.

That is why manual testing, keyboard evaluation, screen reader testing, and usability review are essential parts of our process.

Accessibility Is About Independence

Accessibility is the ability to apply for a job without unnecessary assistance.

To complete an education. To read a document. To understand a video. To complete a form. To participate in a meeting. To contribute to a community.

Accessibility creates independence. Independence creates opportunity. Opportunity changes lives.

Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is never finished.

Technology changes. Standards evolve. Assistive technology improves. People discover new barriers and better solutions.

Open Door Design views accessibility as an ongoing commitment to learning, listening, improving, and growing.

Tell Us When We Miss Something

We know we will not get everything right.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier while using this website or any Open Door Design resource, please tell us.

Accessibility reports, usability feedback, requests for accessible alternatives, and suggestions for improvement are always welcome.
