Accessibility Statement

Accessibility Commitment

Open Door Design is committed to improving accessibility, usability, and inclusive access for users with disabilities and assistive technology needs.

Accessibility is considered throughout planning, content structure, design, testing, and usability evaluation.

Accessibility Approach

This site and related projects are designed with attention to:

  • Logical heading structure.
  • Keyboard accessibility.
  • Screen reader compatibility.
  • Descriptive navigation and meaningful link text.
  • Simplified layouts.
  • Usability-focused interaction design.

Testing frequently includes screen reader workflows using JAWS and keyboard-only navigation.

Standards and Testing

Open Door Design uses WCAG 2.2 AA as a primary reference point while also considering real-world usability, assistive technology behavior, and task completion.

Because automated tools cannot identify every accessibility barrier, manual testing and screen reader evaluation are important parts of the review process.

Known Platform Limitations

Some portfolio versions may be built or demonstrated in Google Sites. Google Sites can limit direct control over semantic HTML, ARIA attributes, keyboard behavior, and advanced interaction patterns.

When platform limitations are present, Open Door Design reduces barriers through simplified layouts, strong heading structure, clear navigation, descriptive links, and accessibility-conscious content organization.

Feedback and Accessibility Assistance

If an accessibility barrier is encountered while using this site or related materials, feedback is welcome.
