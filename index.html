Open Door Design

Opening Doors Through Accessibility, Education, Innovation, and Community

Open Door Design is a growing accessibility initiative dedicated to developing accessibility-focused tools, learning systems, usability resources, and collaborative solutions centered around screen reader users, keyboard accessibility, assistive technology support, and inclusive interaction design.

This site showcases current initiatives, accessibility research, educational resources, innovation projects, and collaborative ideas designed to improve digital accessibility, independence, and quality of life.

Help Build the Future of Open Door Design

Every organization begins with a single idea.

Open Door Design began with a belief that accessibility should create opportunity, independence, dignity, and inclusion for everyone.

What started as one person's journey through accessibility education and lived experience has grown into a vision for something much larger: a collaborative community where accessibility professionals, assistive technology users, developers, designers, educators, researchers, apprentices, organizations, and advocates work together to create practical solutions that improve lives.

Open Door Design is still in its formative stages. The projects featured throughout this website represent ideas in progress, educational initiatives, accessibility engineering concepts, document accessibility workflows, accessible media solutions, and learning systems designed to remove barriers and expand opportunity.

None of those ideas will reach their full potential through one individual alone. The strongest accessibility solutions emerge when people with different experiences and perspectives share their knowledge, challenge assumptions, and build together.

"Every great accessibility solution begins with someone willing to open a door for another person."

Open Door Design exists today because others chose to invest in opportunity.

The Carroll Center for the Blind introduced the possibilities of accessibility through Screen Reader User Tester Training and demonstrated how technology can create independence, confidence, and opportunity.

The Blind Institute of Technology expanded that foundation by investing in education, mentorship, and professional growth through its Digital Accessibility Apprenticeship Program. Through BIT, I developed skills in the Salesforce ecosystem, project management, accessibility auditing, stakeholder engagement, client communication, and earned my CPACC certification. More importantly, BIT helped me believe that accessibility could become more than a career. It could become a mission.

Open Door Design is my opportunity to repay the investment others made in me by helping open doors for the next person.

The goal is not simply to create software or perform accessibility reviews. The goal is to build a collaborative community where ideas become projects, projects become solutions, and solutions improve independence and quality of life for people everywhere.

If you believe accessibility should be practical, inclusive, community-driven, and built alongside the people it serves, your knowledge, lived experience, ideas, and collaboration can help shape what Open Door Design becomes.

Learning together. Building together. Opening doors together.

Join the conversation and help build the future of Open Door Design.

Open Door Design exists today because someone opened a door for us. Our mission is to hold that door open for everyone who follows.

Accessibility and Usability Approach

Accessibility is treated as a practical usability requirement, not a final decoration added after design. Open Door Design work emphasizes clear structure, understandable content, keyboard access, screen reader compatibility, and efficient interaction patterns.

  • Use headings to create a reliable page outline.
  • Use descriptive links that make sense out of context.
  • Support keyboard and screen reader workflows from the beginning.
  • Reduce unnecessary complexity in navigation, forms, media, and learning tools.
  • Document platform limitations honestly when full accessibility control is not available.

Future OpenDoorDesign.org Direction

The long-term goal is to continue developing Open Door Design as a professional accessibility initiative that supports accessibility education, inclusive technology strategy, usability testing, accessible media concepts, practical accessibility engineering, and community-driven learning opportunities.

Future demonstrations will include expanded accessible carousel examples that show how commercial websites can use images, video, links, and form elements without excluding screen reader, keyboard, and mobile assistive technology users.
