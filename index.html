Open Door Design

Accessible learning, practical tools, and ideas built in public.

Follow the Journey

Open Door Design is growing in public. The CPACC Accessible Academy, CPACC Accessible Lab, WCAG Accessible Academy, WCAG Accessible Lab, Beepball Practice Caller, and Open Door Accessible Assistant are available now in different stages of development, testing, and refinement.

Follow the Journey for current milestones, lessons learned, project updates, and the next steps across the Open Door Design ecosystem.

Open for Exploration and Critique

Open Door Design and its projects remain under active development, but the work is not hidden behind a construction sign. All current learning content, labs, demonstrations, and applications are available for people to explore, use, test, and critique.

Some experiences are mature, while others are still revealing what they may become. Feedback from disabled users, accessibility professionals, educators, developers, testers, and curious visitors helps shape every next step.

Our Commitment

  • Accessibility should be designed from the beginning.
  • Screen reader users are designers.
  • Independence drives innovation.
  • Learning and building belong together.
  • Communities create better solutions than individuals.
  • Accessibility is never finished.
  • Every barrier removed creates opportunity.
  • Every open door creates possibility.
