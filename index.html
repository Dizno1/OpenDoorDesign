Our Commitment

  • Accessibility should be designed from the beginning.
  • Screen reader users are designers.
  • Independence drives innovation.
  • Learning and building belong together.
  • Communities create better solutions than individuals.
  • Accessibility is never finished.
  • Every barrier removed creates opportunity.
  • Every open door creates possibility.
